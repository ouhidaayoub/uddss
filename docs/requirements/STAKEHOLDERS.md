# Stakeholders

## Primary Stakeholder

### Head of Department

The Head of Department is the primary stakeholder of the system.

The Head of Department is responsible for department-level decision-making and has the final authority regarding official information, reports, indicators, and statistics.

The Head of Department can approve or reject the adoption and use of the system.

---

## Secondary Stakeholders

### Deputy Head of Department

The Deputy Head supports department management and decision-making activities.

The Deputy Head frequently requests and consumes academic and administrative information.

### Program / Specialty Heads

Program Heads are responsible for monitoring and evaluating the academic performance of their respective programs.

They require reliable information for analysis, planning, and operational activities.

---

## Operational Stakeholders

### Department Secretary

The Secretary participates in the collection, preparation, verification, and management of departmental information.

The Secretary is both a producer and a consumer of information.

### Administrative Staff

Administrative Staff participate in data collection, preparation, validation, and operational reporting activities.

They are responsible for much of the manual effort currently required to answer information requests.

---

## External Stakeholders

### Faculty Administration

Faculty Administration may request information, reports, statistics, and indicators from the department.

### University Administration

University Administration may request information, reports, statistics, and indicators from the department.

---

## Data Producers

The primary producers of departmental information are:

* Head of Department
* Deputy Head of Department
* Program / Specialty Heads
* Secretary
* Administrative Staff

---

## Information Consumers

The primary consumers of departmental information are:

* Head of Department
* Deputy Head of Department
* Program / Specialty Heads
* Secretary
* Faculty Administration
* University Administration

---

## System Users

The stakeholders expected to interact directly with the system are:

* Head of Department
* Deputy Head of Department
* Program / Specialty Heads
* Secretary
* Administrative Staff

---

## Authority Hierarchy

When disagreements occur regarding official departmental information, authority follows the hierarchy below:

1. Head of Department
2. Deputy Head of Department
3. Program / Specialty Heads

The Head of Department has the final authority regarding official departmental information and reporting.

## Stakeholder Summary

| Stakeholder               | Role                                                                | Goal                                                                                   | Power  |
| -------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------ |
| Head of Department        | Primary stakeholder and final decision-maker                       | Obtain reliable information for decision-making, planning, monitoring, and reporting   | High   |
| Deputy Head of Department | Supports department management and operations                     | Access reliable information for management, analysis, and coordination activities     | High   |
| Program / Specialty Heads | Responsible for monitoring and managing academic programs          | Analyze program performance, monitor indicators, and support academic planning        | Medium |
| Department Secretary      | Produces, prepares, verifies, and manages departmental information | Efficiently prepare and provide accurate information when requested                   | Medium |
| Administrative Staff      | Collect, process, validate, and organize departmental information  | Reduce manual effort and produce reliable information efficiently                     | Medium |
| Faculty Administration    | Requests information from the department                           | Obtain reliable reports, statistics, and indicators from the department               | Low    |
| University Administration | Requests information from the department                           | Obtain reliable reports, statistics, and indicators for institutional decision-making | Low    |
